1. Scope of data collection

Delfi Check-in collects required information to provide event registration and check-in services, including full name, email, phone number, organization, and event-defined custom fields.

2. Purpose of processing

  • Operate registration/check-in flows and send event-related confirmations/notifications.
  • Provide reports and statistics under role-based access control.
  • Improve service quality and resolve support requests.

3. Legal basis and data subject rights

We process personal data only for declared purposes with access control and audit logging. Data subjects may request access, rectification, restriction, or erasure in accordance with applicable laws.

4. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on controlled infrastructure with permission boundaries and activity logging.
  • Web traffic is protected by appropriate security mechanisms (for example HTTPS and authenticated sessions).
  • Retention period depends on subscribed plans, customer policies, and legal requirements.

5. Third-party sharing

Delfi Check-in does not sell personal data. Sharing with third parties (if any) is limited to service operation or legal obligations, under proper confidentiality terms.

6. Privacy contact

For privacy or personal-data requests, please contact us via the owner information shown in the website footer.
