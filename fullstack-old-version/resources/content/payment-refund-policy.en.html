1. Payment principles

Delfi Check-in SaaS is provided under subscribed plans and billing cycles defined in quotations/contracts. Timely payment is required to maintain uninterrupted service.

2. Activation and renewal

  • Service plans are activated after successful payment confirmation.
  • Service validity follows the contract/order cycle.
  • Renewal should be completed before expiration to avoid interruption.

3. Refund policy

  • Paid fees are generally non-refundable after activation and resource provisioning.
  • Any refund request is reviewed case-by-case based on contract terms, usage level, and issue cause.
  • For severe provider-side incidents, both parties will handle remediation according to SLA/service commitments.

4. Service cancellation

Customers may request cancellation via official email/written notice. Cancellation effectiveness depends on the agreed contractual conditions.

5. Billing support

For invoice, payment, or refund support, please contact the owner information shown in the website footer.
